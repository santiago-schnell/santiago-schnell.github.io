# Research

Santiago believes that there is a continuum between health and disease. If we are capable of measuring this continuum, 
we can revolutionize early disease detection, deepen our understanding of underlying mechanisms, and develop more 
precise interventions.

Santiago works with a multidisciplinary research team at the intersection of biophysical chemistry, mathematical 
and statistical modeling, and pathophysiology. Through his diverse research projects, Santiago and his team leverage 
their interdisciplinary perspective to provide innovative solutions to measure physical constants of biochemical 
reactions, develop cutting-edge diagnostic tools, and design more effective therapeutic strategies.

## Principles of Enzyme and Biochemical Kinetics

!!! project-summary
    *We develop standard-based approaches for measuring enzyme kinetic parameters and deriving rate equations that 
    distinguish the mechanisms of enzyme-catalyzed and other biochemical reactions.*

<div class="project-section">

<figure class="project-figure float-left">
  <img src="../assets/images/research/principles-of-enzyme-kinetics.jpg" alt="Michaelis-Menten Variance Heatmap" />
  <figcaption class="figure-caption">
    Computed parameter variance for noisy progress curve experiments.
    <u>Source</u>: Wylie Stroberg and Santiago Schnell (2016)
    <em>Biophysical Chemistry</em> <strong>219</strong>, 12–27.
  </figcaption>
</figure>

<div class="project-text">
<p>We combine chemical kinetics with mathematical, computational, and statistical methods to develop standard-based 
approaches, which can distinguish molecular mechanisms under various experimental conditions.</p>

<p>Our research focuses on deriving mathematical approximations of rate law equations that govern the progress curves 
of enzyme-catalyzed and other biochemical reactions. This involves implementing advanced mathematical techniques for 
scaling and perturbation analysis. After deriving these approximations, we assess their effectiveness in modeling 
progress curves and estimating kinetic parameters under different experimental conditions. In addition, we develop 
and implement algorithms to accurately estimate kinetic parameters directly from progress curve experiments.</p>

<p>Our work in enzyme and biochemical kinetics has broad implications for understanding the fundamental principles 
of biochemical reactions and can inform the development of clinical test, novel therapeutics and biotechnological 
applications.</p>
</div>

</div>

## Developing Standards for Reporting Enzymology Data

!!! project-summary
    *We are developing standards for data reporting enzyme functional data with the aim to improve the quality of data 
    published in the scientific literature.*

<div class="project-section">

<figure class="project-figure float-right">
  <img src="../assets/images/research/strenda.jpg" alt="STRENDA logo" />
  <figcaption class="figure-caption">Logo for the "Standards for Reporting Enzymology Data" Project.
</figcaption>
</figure>

<div class="project-text">
<p>We are developing <a href="https://www.beilstein-institut.de/en/projects/strenda/">Standards for REporting ENzymology 
DAta</a> (STRENDA) in collaboration with an <a href="https://www.beilstein-institut.de/en/projects/strenda/commission/">international 
committee</a> sponsored by the <a href="https://www.beilstein-institut.de/en/">Beilstein-Institut</a>.</p>

<p>The <a href="https://www.beilstein-institut.de/en/projects/strenda/guidelines/">STRENDA Guidelines</a> are developed 
through extensive interactions with the biochemistry community to define the minimum information that is needed to 
correctly describe assay conditions (<a href="https://www.beilstein-institut.de/en/projects/strenda/guidelines#block-4017">List 
Level 1A</a>) and enzyme activity data (<a href="https://www.beilstein-institut.de/en/projects/strenda/guidelines#block-4018">List 
Level 1B</a>).  STRENDA aims to ensure that data sets are complete and validated, allowing scientists to review, reuse 
and verify them. The emphasis is on providing useful and reliable information.</p>
</div>

</div>

### STRENDA Database

Standards for REporting ENzymology DAta DataBase (<a href="https://www.beilstein-strenda-db.org/strenda/">STRENDA DB</a>) 
is a validation and storage system for enzyme function data that incorporates the STRENDA Guidelines. It provides authors 
who are preparing a manuscript with a user-friendly, web-based service that checks automatically enzymology data sets 
entered in the submission form that they are complete and valid before they are submitted as part of a publication to 
a journal.

## EnzymeML: An Open Standard for Sharing and Standardizing Enzyme Kinetics Data

!!! project-summary
    *The EnzymeML language is an open standard based on the XML markup language.*

<div class="project-section">

<figure class="project-figure float-left">
  <img src="../assets/images/research/enzymeml.jpg" alt="EnzymeML code" />
  <figcaption class="figure-caption">Logo for the "Screenshot of EnzymeML XML markup.
</figcaption>
</figure>

<div class="project-text">
<p><a href="http://enzymeml.org/">EnzymeML</a> is a free, open-standard XML-based markup interchange format for 
standardizing the monitoring and exchange of enzyme kinetic data. It allows scientists to share experimental protocols, 
results, and kinetic data even if they are using different instruments, electronic laboratory notebooks, or databases.</p>

<p>EnzymeML follows the <a href="https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/">FAIR Principles</a> (Findable, Accessible, 
Interoperable, Reusable), ensuring data is easily located, accessed, combined, and reused. It is compatible with 
the <a href="http://sbml.org/">Systems Biology Markup Language</a>, and is supported by 
the <a href="https://www.beilstein-institut.de/en/projects/strenda/commission/">STRENDA Commission</a>. The format
continues to be evolved and expanded by an international community.</p>

<p>Development is jointly led by <a href="http://www.itb.uni-stuttgart.de/people/pleiss.juergen">Juergen Pleiss</a>,
<a href="https://www.cos.uni-heidelberg.de/en/research-groups/modelling-of-biological-processes/dr-frank-bergmann">Frank T. Bergmann</a>, 
and Santiago Schnell. It is available in <a href="https://github.com/EnzymeML">GitHub</a>.</p>

<p>By promoting data standardization and accessibility, <a href="http://enzymeml.org/">EnzymeML</a> aims to accelerate
research in enzyme kinetics and biocatalysis, streamline collaborations, and ultimately contribute to advancements in 
drug discovery,biotechnology, and our understanding of biological and chemical systems.</p>
</div>

## Measuring and Improving Research University Performance

!!! project-summary 
    *We are developing new metrics and models to measure and improve performance of scientists, research universities, 
    and academic policies.*

<div class="project-section">

<figure class="project-figure float-right">
  <img src="../assets/images/research/science-of-science.jpg" alt="Network illustration of scientific collaboration" />
  <figcaption class="figure-caption">Science is a network: a few highly connected nodes shape how ideas,
  people, and resources flow.</figcaption>
</figure>

<div class="project-text">
<p>Modern research universities are complex ecosystems, requiring a deep understanding of their inner workings to make 
informed policy and administrative decisions. Scientometrics, the quantitative study of scientific research, and 
economic modeling provide the tools
to achieve this.</p>

<p>Our goal is to develop models that leverage large-scale scientometric and economic data to optimize the use of 
limited resources, ultimately maximizing research output and societal impact. We investigate the ongoing cost-benefit 
decisions academic institutions make as they compete for resources and reputation, as well as how scientists respond 
to incentives and costs related to research funding, scientific quality, and reputation.</p>

<p>By understanding these dynamics, we aim to inform strategies that enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of 
research universities, foster a thriving academic environment, and ultimately contribute to the advancement of 
knowledge and innovation.</p>
</div>
